PROJECT AETHER · PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy, in plain language.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains the information handled by the Project Aether website and online community. Project Aether is published by Project Aether. For privacy questions or requests, email kiwisystems-ee@outlook.com.

Information we handle

  • Account details: email address, account and session identifiers, Minecraft username, and the self-declared age group selected during signup. We do not ask for a birth date or verify age; this choice does not change features.
  • Community activity: posts, replies, likes, and associated timestamps and account identifiers. Community posts and usernames are visible to other community visitors.
  • Security verification: Cloudflare Turnstile processes technical information to help prevent automated signups. Supabase provides authentication and the community database.
  • Session IP address: Supabase Auth records IP addresses for successful account sessions. The Project Aether launcher displays up to 100 sessions from the last 30 days to the Project Aether Owner for account-safety checks. This view does not include anonymous page visits or failed sign-in attempts, and Project Aether does not keep a separate copy of these IP records.
  • Browser storage: the website stores an authentication session in local storage so you stay signed in. Signing out clears that session from this browser.

How information is used and shared

We use account and community information to provide sign-in, profiles, discussions, replies, and likes, and to protect the service from abuse. Session IPs are visible in the launcher only to the Project Aether Owner; Supabase, as the authentication provider, also processes and stores them, and its authorized operators may access data as needed to provide the service. Supabase and Cloudflare process information needed to provide their respective services. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising. We do not collect your local Minecraft worlds through this website.

Retention and your choices

Community content remains available while it is stored in the service. The launcher displays session IPs from the last 30 days, up to 100 records; Supabase retains underlying authentication sessions according to its session lifecycle and project configuration. To ask about account or content deletion, or to request access or correction where applicable, contact kiwisystems-ee@outlook.com. Signing out only clears the browser session; it does not delete your online account or posts.

Children and age information

Signup asks whether you are under 18 or 18 or over. This is self-declared, is not age verification, and does not affect features. If you are under 18, please review this policy with a parent or guardian if you need help understanding it.

Changes

We may update this policy as the website changes. The current update date appears at the top of this page.
